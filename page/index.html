How this was built, and what it cannot tell you

Method

  • Seats. Each formula is rounded with the Webster (Sainte-Laguë) method. States under six million people keep at least their current seats, following the Article 81 proviso.
  • Votes. 2024 Lok Sabha results for all 543 seats, pooled into the alliances that actually shared seats in each state. Each constituency's vote is spread over its assembly segments; in eleven states the latest assembly election supplies each segment's lean relative to its constituency.
  • Neutral maps. For twenty states, GerryChain's ReCom chain builds contiguous constituencies from whole assembly segments: four chains of 2,000 recorded plans for every seat count tested.
  • Drawn maps. Short-burst optimisation searches for the plan with the most NDA seats, and separately the fewest. "Tilted" is the 5th or 95th percentile of the neutral ensemble: a map an outlier test would not flag.
  • Scoring. Ordered-margin plots, efficiency gap and mean–median difference follow the ensemble-outlier approach of Duchin and the MGGG lab.

Limits

  • One election. Alliances have shifted since 2024, and voters respond to new boundaries and candidates.
  • A neutral redraw of today's 543 seats gives the NDA about eight more seats than the real map did in 2024. Some of that is chance in one election and some is smoothing where segment data is missing, so compare each formula with the "Freeze at 543" row, not with 293.
  • Building blocks are whole assembly segments, with constituencies allowed to differ by about 20% in size. A real commission redraws segments too and can cut finer, so the optimised figures are a floor on what is possible, not a ceiling.
  • Nine states have no segment-level lean in this data (Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Chhattisgarh, Himachal Pradesh). Where one side swept such a state, no map can change the result here, though one could in reality.
  • Assam, Jammu and Kashmir and sixteen small states and territories (39 seats) are not redrawn; they keep their 2024 split.
  • Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and women is ignored. The reported Reddy and Tharoor formulas are sketches; the parameters here are one reading of each.
  • "Optimised" is the best plan a heuristic found. It is not evidence that anyone would draw it. A study of the 2008 delimitation found it largely politically neutral; the Assam and Jammu and Kashmir exercises of 2022–23 drew gerrymandering allegations that the authorities reject.
